# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support technical execution by aligning engineering capacity, team health, and delivery quality with the broader project plan. They act as the operational bridge between product needs and the engineering team.

### Responsibilities
- Own engineering delivery health, staffing, and capacity planning
- Support developers with prioritization, mentoring, and technical decision-making
- Coordinate work sequencing with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Help identify delivery risks, bottlenecks, and resourcing gaps
- Maintain visibility into sprint health, dependencies, and release readiness

### Goals
- Deliver reliable engineering execution
- Improve team productivity and delivery predictability
- Balance technical quality with business timelines and scope

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and staffing discussions
- Weekly delivery reviews with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Technical risk updates and escalations during milestones or releases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to support team execution and career growth
- Collaborates with Product Managers on roadmap trade-offs and delivery feasibility
- Coordinates with Project Managers on schedule, scope, and dependency planning

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads own quality validation, test strategy, and release readiness. They ensure that team output meets acceptance criteria and customer expectations before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage approach, and quality gates
- Lead defect triage, regression planning, and test execution
- Validate that features meet business and technical acceptance criteria
- Partner with Developers on testability and defect prevention
- Support milestone and release readiness decisions

### Goals
- Reduce defect escape and production risk
- Establish consistent quality expectations across the project
- Improve confidence in launch readiness and user experience

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions and test review meetings
- Defect triage with Developers and Product Managers
- Release readiness sign-off with Project Managers and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to ensure bug fixes are validated and regressions are prevented
- Supports Product Managers by validating whether requirements are met and risks are understood
- Provides Project Managers with quality status and release confidence updates

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners help teams identify security, privacy, and policy risks early. They ensure that delivery decisions comply with organizational standards and regulatory obligations.

### Responsibilities
- Review security risks, threat models, and exposure areas
- Ensure compliance requirements and control checks are incorporated into delivery work
- Partner with engineering teams on secure design and remediation guidance
- Support risk assessment for changes impacting data, access, or external integrations
- Contribute to incident response and post-release security follow-up when needed

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Improve trust and resilience in project delivery
- Ensure the solution meets policy, privacy, and governance requirements

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and architecture check-ins
- Compliance and risk assessments during planning and release phases
- Incident communication support and remediation coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure implementation patterns and control requirements
- Partners with Project Managers on risk tracking and mitigation planning
- Supports Product Managers by highlighting regulatory or customer-impact risks tied to roadmap decisions

---

## Release Managers / Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Delivery Leads coordinate deployment activities, release windows, and communication. They reduce delivery risk by planning and monitoring the transition from development to production.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules, staging checks, and go-live plans
- Manage deployment dependencies, rollback readiness, and communication flows
- Align release activities with QA, engineering, and stakeholder expectations
- Track launch readiness and operational risk before critical releases
- Support communication for feature launches and incident recovery where needed

### Goals
- Minimize deployment disruption and release risk
- Improve predictability for production rollouts
- Ensure clear operational ownership around go-live activities

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment checklists
- Go/no-go reviews with engineering, QA, and stakeholders
- Post-release status updates and rollback coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Project Managers to align release calendars and milestones
- Coordinates with Developers and QA to confirm deployment readiness and smoke-test coverage
- Supports Product Managers with launch planning and stakeholder communication

---

## Customer Success / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Representatives connect delivery work to the real-world experience of users and customers. They bring feedback, adoption concerns, and support readiness needs into project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Represent customer pain points, support issues, and service expectations
- Provide feedback on usability, operational readiness, and adoption barriers
- Coordinate support readiness plans for new features or changes
- Share insights from support and customer conversations with Product and Engineering teams
- Help assess the impact of releases and post-launch issues

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure delivered value is sustainable in real operating conditions
- Reduce avoidable support burden after launch

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback review sessions
- Support readiness and issue triage conversations
- Stakeholder updates on customer impact and adoption trends

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with real-world user and support insights
- Helps Developers understand operational friction and usability gaps
- Shares risk and readiness information with Project Managers and Release Leads

---

## Data / Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Owners define and monitor the metrics used to evaluate whether a project is delivering the intended value. They help teams make decisions based on measurable outcomes rather than assumptions.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and measurement frameworks
- Track adoption, quality, performance, and business impact indicators
- Support experiments, analysis, and continuous improvement efforts
- Partner with Product Managers and stakeholders on reporting and insight interpretation
- Help validate whether project goals are being met after launch

### Goals
- Measure customer and business value objectively
- Improve evidence-based decision making
- Support continuous improvement and learning across teams

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and dashboard reporting
- Outcome analysis and roadmap evaluation discussions
- Post-launch learning and retrospective inputs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define and track meaningful success metrics
- Supports Project Managers and stakeholders with status reporting and trend analysis
- Shares evidence with Developers and QA to validate the effectiveness of delivered changes

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles reinforce the need for clear ownership, cross-functional collaboration, and practical accountability during project planning, execution, and release.
